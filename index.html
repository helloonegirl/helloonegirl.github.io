my story

Growing programmes, and people, with heart.

ClearPlans, budgets and reports that anyone can follow.
CaringI listen first and work gently with people and communities.
ReliableDeadlines met, details checked, nothing left hanging.

My journey started in classrooms. Since 2018 I've taught English, Maths, Arts and Economics in learning centres around Klang, many of them serving refugee children. Teaching showed me how much good structure and a little encouragement can change a life.

Over time I moved into running programmes. I've coordinated a refugee advocacy organisation, led a leadership camp for youth from eight countries, delivered higher-education programmes at Fugee, and handled finance and operations for a UK social enterprise.

In 2025 I founded SEHER, a youth-led organisation for refugee youth in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor. The name means dawn in Urdu, and it's a reminder of why I do this work.

I also love standing in front of a room. I speak at refugee programmes as a motivational speaker, and I run workshops on advocacy, child safety, digital safety and finding a path to university.

Today I bring all of that to my clients: careful planning, clear writing, honest numbers and warmth in how I work with people.
