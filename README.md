# Areej Khan · just one of you

Personal website of Areej Khan: programme manager, grant writer, tutor and website maker, based in Kuala Lumpur.

Live at **https://helloonegirl.github.io**

- **About me**: story, skills, portfolio and certificates
- **Services**: grant writing, donor reports & M&E, project leadership, operations & bookkeeping, websites for little shops, translation, and private tutoring (prices in RM with a USD switch)
- **Fun corner**: a teddy welcome, a mind-reading trick and little games

Everything lives in `index.html` (prices and service details are in the `SERVICES` list near the bottom) and the `assets/` folder.
